ABOUT

CHUNG & CO. brings deep expertise across brand strategy, identity, and creative direction — for independent designer labels and global fashion houses alike. We offer a rare breadth of perspective: from strategic planning and brand positioning to collection design, art direction, and the full spectrum of brand experience.

We uncover the essence of a brand and architect it into strategy and creative direction as one integrated system — connecting planning, design, production, space, and marketing into a single, coherent flow that reaches the consumer as one complete narrative.

KATIE
CHUNG

Founder & Creative Director, CHUNG & CO.

Studied womenswear at Central Saint Martins before building a career in menswear design. Joined Wooyoungmi in 2012 as Art Director and led the brand through a decade of creative transformation — serving as sole Creative Director from 2014 to 2020.

From 2022 to 2026, Chief Creative Officer at MCM — overseeing global brand identity, collection design, and creative direction across all categories and markets.

Founded CHUNG & CO. to build a studio where strategy and creativity work as one: from identity and collection to events, spaces, and the full arc of brand experience.
